# Play Store listing (draft)

**Title (≤30):** CallerX — SIP Dialer & Calls

**Short description (≤80):** A clean dialer that also makes SIP/VoIP calls, with recording and backup.

**Full description:**

CallerX is a fast, privacy-friendly phone app: it works as your everyday dialer for SIM calls
and adds SIP/VoIP calling on top of it.

• Dialer that feels familiar — Truecaller-style keypad, contacts, call log with grouping
• SIP accounts (UDP/TCP/TLS/WSS) with TLS and SRTP options, multiple accounts, per-call choice
• Call features: hold, mute, speaker, DTMF keypad, call waiting, blind & attended transfer,
  conference, call forwarding (always/busy/no answer)
• Recording with a built-in player (seek, 0.5×–2× speed, share, delete)
• Missed-call auto-SMS, voicemail shortcut, wildcard call blocking, block-before-ringing
• Call-quality report (MOS estimate, jitter, packet loss, delay, SRTP suite)
• Backups to Google Drive, Dropbox or phone storage — optional AES-256-GCM password
• Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile, AMOLED theme, text-size control

No ads. No analytics. Your call history, recordings and backups stay on your device unless you
share them.

**Data safety answers (draft):**
- Data collected: none by the developer.
- Data shared: none. Backups are written to a folder the user chooses (SAF).
- Permissions used: contacts, call log, phone, microphone, SMS (only for the optional
  missed-call auto-reply), notifications, Bluetooth (headsets).

**Screenshots to capture:** Dialer, call screen, call waiting card, settings, recordings, widget.
